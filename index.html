    How therapy works.

    No timetable to fit into. Four steps, at the pace your body sets — and we keep adjusting as it changes.

    1

    Talk to us

    Call or message. Tell us what hurts, what you have tried and what your doctor has said.

    2

    Consultation & assessment

    An unhurried first session. We look at posture, breath and movement, and read your reports.

    3

    Your plan

    A practice designed around your body and guided by Ramya — not a routine you squeeze into.

    4

    Review & adjust

    We check progress and change the plan as you get stronger, steadier and calmer.

    Questions.

    Where is Samrakshana Yoga Kendra?

    At No 13/11, First Cross Street, Raja Annamalaipuram (R.A. Puram), Chennai 600 028, close to Mylapore, Mandaveli and Alwarpet.

    Who teaches at Samrakshana Yoga Kendra?

    Classes and yoga therapy are led by founder R. Ramya, who has an MSc in Yoga, is a QCI Level 6 Yoga Therapist and trained at Krishnamacharya Yoga Mandiram.

    What sessions are offered?

    Private one-to-one sessions, group sessions, pranayama, meditation and healing chants, for children, adults and seniors.

    Do I need to be flexible or fit to start?

    No. Practice begins from where your body is today and is adjusted for age, health and history.

    Does yoga therapy replace medical treatment?

    No. Yoga therapy supports and complements the care you receive from your doctor. Please share any medical reports at your first visit.

    Are there online classes?

    Yes. Students join both in person and online, including from other time zones. Message on WhatsApp for current timings.

    How do I book?

    Message on WhatsApp or call +91 86101 25195. Messages from the website arrive already written, so you only need to press send.
