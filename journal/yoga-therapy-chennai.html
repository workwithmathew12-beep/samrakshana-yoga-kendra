What is yoga therapy, and how is it different from a yoga class?

A plain guide to yoga therapy in Chennai: who it suits, what happens at a first session at Samrakshana Yoga Kendra in R.A. Puram, and what to expect.

Samrakshana Yoga KendraR.A. Puram, Chennai4 min read21 October 2026

Most people meet yoga in a class: one teacher at the front, a room of students following the same sequence. That works well for many people. But if you live with back pain, are recovering from an injury, are older, or simply feel unsure of your body, a shared sequence may not fit you. That is where yoga therapy comes in.

Yoga therapy starts with the person, not the pose

In yoga therapy, the practice is designed around one individual. The teacher first learns about your health history, your daily routine, any reports from your doctor, and what you want to be able to do. The postures, breathing and relaxation that follow are chosen and adapted for you, and they change as you change.

This idea sits at the heart of the Krishnamacharya tradition, the Chennai-rooted lineage in which R. Ramya, founder of Samrakshana Yoga Kendra, trained at Krishnamacharya Yoga Mandiram.

Who yoga therapy can help

  • People living with stiffness or pain in the back, neck, shoulders or knees
  • Seniors who want to stay mobile, steady and confident
  • People managing long-term conditions such as diabetes or high blood pressure, alongside their doctor's treatment
  • Children and adults with special needs
  • Anyone dealing with stress, anxiety or poor sleep

What happens at a first session

A first session is a conversation as much as a practice. Expect to talk about your health and routine, to be observed gently as you move and breathe, and to leave with a short practice you can begin straight away. Bring any recent medical reports. You do not need to be flexible, fit or experienced.

Yoga therapy and medical care

Yoga therapy supports the care you receive from your doctor; it does not replace it. If you are under treatment, keep your doctor informed, and tell your yoga therapist about any medication, surgery or diagnosis.

Quick answers

Is yoga therapy the same as a yoga class?

No. A class follows one sequence for everyone; yoga therapy is planned around one person and adjusted over time.

Do I need experience?

No. Yoga therapy begins from where your body is today.
