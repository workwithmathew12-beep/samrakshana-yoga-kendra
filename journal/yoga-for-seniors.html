Starting yoga after 60: what to know before your first class

Balance, joints, breath and confidence: how older students in R.A. Puram and Mylapore begin yoga safely at Samrakshana Yoga Kendra, Chennai.

Samrakshana Yoga KendraR.A. Puram, Chennai3 min read21 October 2026

It is never too late to start yoga. Many students at Samrakshana Yoga Kendra began in their fifties, sixties or later, and several have practised with R. Ramya for years.

What yoga offers older bodies

  • Balance and steadiness, which matter more with every year
  • Joint mobility in the hips, knees, shoulders and spine
  • Strength for everyday tasks like climbing stairs or getting up from the floor
  • Calmer breathing and better rest
  • Company: a regular class is also a community

Starting safely

Tell your teacher about any conditions, surgeries or medication, especially for blood pressure, diabetes, heart health or joints. Expect props such as chairs, blocks and bolsters; they are there to make practice safe and comfortable, not easier. Move within comfort, and never hold your breath.

Booking for a parent

Many adult children, some living abroad, book yoga for their parents. If that is you, message first: we will explain what a first session involves and how practice is adapted for older bodies.

Quick answers

Is yoga safe after 60?

For most people, yes, when it is adapted to their health. Share your medical history and check with your doctor if you are unsure.

Can I use a chair?

Yes. Chairs and other props are used whenever they help.
